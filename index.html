About Quillan

Quillan Salkilld was born on 28 December 1999 in Pinjarra, Western Australia. He started training in March 2018, turned pro in 2021, and lost his pro debut. He has not lost since.

He earned his UFC contract with a unanimous decision win over Gauge Young on Dana White's Contender Series in September 2024. Since his UFC debut in February 2025 he has won all six of his fights, and five of them ended in the first round.

He trains at Luistro Combat Academy and fights at 155 pounds. In August 2026 he beat Mateusz Gamrot in his first UFC main event, and he has said he wants a top-five opponent next.
